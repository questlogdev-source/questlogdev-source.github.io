Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Quest Log Dev Pipeline ("the app") is a private, single-user tool operated by the owner of the Quest Log Dev channel. It is not offered to the public.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload and schedule videos to the owner's own channel and to read that channel's video statistics and comments.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to manage and analyze the owner's own channel. OAuth tokens and statistics are stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.
